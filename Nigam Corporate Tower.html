Overview

A Premier Commercial
Address in Gomti Nagar

Nigam Corporate Tower is an exclusive commercial investment opportunity in the heart of Lucknow's fastest-growing business corridor, Vibhuti Khand. Positioned opposite Omaxe Courtyard in Gomti Nagar, the corridor has emerged as the city's premier commercial address, attracting banks, multi-national companies, consultancies, and premium office tenants.

Designed for premium corporate occupancy, the tower offers flexible office spaces from 400 to 800 sqft, served by a premium passenger elevator for effortless, comfortable access to every floor — brought together with modern infrastructure, safety compliance, and future-ready facilities. A fast-track construction schedule puts your space on the path to rental readiness within just 1 year.
