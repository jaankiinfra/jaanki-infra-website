Detailed Specifications

Structure & Foundation

Strong RCC frame structure with 14 columns (pillars) and 13 hanging (latak) beams. Foundation 4.5 ft beneath ground level and 2.5 ft above ground level. Front Size: 20.37 ft | Depth: 41 ft. East-facing homes.

Flooring & Tiles

Living/Dining/Bedrooms: Superior Quality Vitrified Tiles of SOMANY. Sit-Out: Ceramic Tiles / Granite. Toilets: Vitrified Tiles throughout with Granite top niches.

Kitchen

Furnished Modular Kitchen with excellent quality steel parts. SOMANY vitrified floor tiles and skirting. BERGER high-quality paint on walls and ceiling.

Bathrooms & Sanitary

Branded sanitary fittings of CERA and PARRYWARE throughout. Vitrified wall tiles up to door level (~2000mm). BERGER paint on ceiling. Provision for geyser installation in all toilets.

Electrical & Plumbing

ISI-compliant HAVELLS modular switches, sockets, distribution boxes and circuit breakers. Uncut end-to-end wiring of RR KABEL. ISI-compliant CPVC water supply pipes. Underground sewer line throughout the project.

Doors, Windows & Exteriors

Main Entrance: Painted hardwood frame with flush door shutters. Internal Doors: Painted hardwood frame. Windows: Strong steel frame with mirror glass. Exterior walls: Grade anti-fungal/anti-algal BERGER paint.
