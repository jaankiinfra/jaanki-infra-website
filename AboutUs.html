Our Story

Your Trusted Partner
in Premium Living

Welcome to Jaanki Infra Developers (P) Ltd. — one of Lucknow's most trusted names in residential real estate. We believe that everyone deserves to live in a beautiful, well-built home, and for over a decade we have been turning that belief into reality across Gomti Nagar and Mahanagar.

We offer a comprehensive range of services — from custom home design to luxury construction — with over 10 years of proven experience in the Indian real estate market. We know Lucknow inside out and are unwavering in our commitment to delivering the best possible service to every client.

Building a home is deeply personal. That's why we work collaboratively with our clients, offering a variety of customisation options and keeping them informed at every milestone. We are equally committed to sustainability — using energy-efficient practices and reducing environmental impact across all our projects.
